Biography

A true musical polymath.

Hailing from the Canadian prairies, Mallory Chipman is an award-winning musician, songwriter, producer, and music scholar — celebrated for her acrobatic voice and the way she powerfully weaves truth and story into song. With influences from across genres, her signature sound has been described as “the finest folk-rock currently being made.”

Her 2024 album Songs to a Wild God hit #1 on CKUA, #5 on the Folk Alliance International Charts, and has been featured on NPR and SiriusXM Radio. Over a five-album career, Chipman has toured extensively and internationally, opening for artists including Jann Arden and Madeleine Peyroux.

Reflecting contemporary themes while placing her “amidst the longer tradition of the great singer-songwriters” (Americana UK), her craft has established her as an instant classic in the Canadian folk scene.

Her new EP ‘the time’ was released December 10, 2025 — cementing that “her artistic stature is undeniable.”
