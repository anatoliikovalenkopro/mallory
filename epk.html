Biography

Hailing from the Canadian prairies, Mallory Chipman is a true musical polymath — an award-winning musician, songwriter, producer, and music scholar celebrated for her acrobatic voice and the way she weaves truth and story into song.

Her 2024 album Songs to a Wild God hit #1 on CKUA, #5 on the Folk Alliance International Charts, and was featured on NPR and SiriusXM. Across a five-album career she has toured internationally, opening for Jann Arden and Madeleine Peyroux.

She is currently nominated for Solo Artist of the Year at the 2026 Canadian Folk Music Awards. Her latest EP, the time, arrived December 10, 2025.
